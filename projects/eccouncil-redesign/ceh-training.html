Frequently asked

Do I need the Practical exam to be "certified"?

No. Passing the Knowledge Exam earns the CEH credential. The Practical is optional and earns the higher CEH Master designation on top of it.

Can I switch tiers after I start?

Yes. You can upgrade from On-Demand to Live Online or Unlimited at any time; you'll only pay the difference.
